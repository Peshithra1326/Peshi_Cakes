Our Story

Crafting Sweet Memories Since 2010

Welcome to our artisan cake boutique, where passion meets precision in every creation. Founded by Master Pastry Chef Olivia Bennett, our bakery began as a small family business driven by a love for creating edible works of art.

Each of our cakes is handcrafted using only the finest ingredients - organic eggs, European butter, premium chocolate, and locally sourced seasonal fruits. We believe that exceptional taste comes from exceptional ingredients and meticulous attention to detail.

Olivia Bennett

Master Pastry Chef & Founder
